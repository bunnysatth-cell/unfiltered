This Privacy Policy explains how Unfiltered (“Unfiltered”, “we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use our tool. Unfiltered is a web-based content management tool that uses TikTok OAuth and TikTok APIs to help users draft, prepare, and publish short-form video content that the user explicitly authorizes.

1) Information we collect

We aim to minimize data collection. Depending on how you deploy or operate Unfiltered (for example, through automation platforms like n8n or hosting providers), the following information may be processed:

  • TikTok authorization data: OAuth authorization codes and access tokens required to perform actions you explicitly authorize.
  • Basic TikTok account information (if requested by scope): such as display name, open_id, and avatar URL.
  • Content metadata: video filename, caption text, hashtags, and publish settings you provide.
  • Operational logs: timestamps, request IDs, error logs, IP address, and user-agent (standard server logs).

Unfiltered does not collect, store, or have access to TikTok account passwords at any time. Authentication is handled exclusively through TikTok’s official OAuth authorization system.

2) How we use information

  • To authenticate users via TikTok OAuth and confirm granted permissions
  • To upload or publish videos or create drafts on TikTok as explicitly directed by the user
  • To troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and maintain system security
  • To comply with applicable laws and platform requirements

3) Where data is stored

Unfiltered is designed to operate with minimal data storage. In many configurations, access tokens and logs may be stored within your own automation environment (for example, n8n) or hosting provider. If you self-host Unfiltered, you control where and how data is stored.

4) Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • With TikTok to perform API actions you explicitly authorize
  • With service providers you use to operate Unfiltered (hosting, automation, logging), subject to their own terms
  • For legal or safety reasons if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security

5) Data retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. OAuth access tokens may persist until they expire or until you revoke access. Logs, if enabled, are typically retained for a limited period for debugging and security.

6) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including limiting access and using secure transport (HTTPS) where possible. However, no system is completely secure. If you self-host Unfiltered, you are responsible for securing your environment.

7) Your choices and controls

  • You may revoke Unfiltered’s access at any time via TikTok account settings (OAuth revocation)
  • You may stop using Unfiltered at any time
  • If you self-host, you may delete stored tokens or logs from your own environment

8) Children’s privacy

Unfiltered is not intended for children and must not be used by anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their jurisdiction.

9) International users

If you access Unfiltered from outside the region where it is hosted, information may be processed in other countries depending on your hosting or automation setup.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above reflects the most recent revision.
